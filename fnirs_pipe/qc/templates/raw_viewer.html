Quantitative Metrics

    Original recording — optical density

    The same recording measured over three channel sets. Long is the set the verdict is read off; Short is left uncoloured, a short channel's coupling being high by construction and having no established threshold, so that row answers whether the regressors those channels feed are trustworthy rather than whether the run is usable.

    Motion and spikes

    GVTD is an RMS across channels, so each set is its own trace with its own cutoff. Read the rows down a column, not across one: two sets' shares are counted against different cutoffs and are not two readings of one quantity.

    Channel Quality Summary

    Status / SCI / CV / PSP / SNR per channel, green = pass and red = fail. Status screens on the coupled-window share; SCI, PSP, CV and SNR are reported only.

    Per-trial Quality

    Each event window scored on its own. Colour is relative within a metric row, not a threshold.

    Per-channel Metrics

    One row per source-detector pair: a decision applies to the pair, and SCI is a property of the pair rather than of either wavelength. The per-wavelength numbers are in the channel metrics CSV written beside the quality record. Rows in red were rejected by screening, and Status names the criterion.
